# RAL Scientific Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the scientific progression of the RAL programme. It is explicitly separated from personal career goals or internship plans.

## Phase 1: Measurement and Baseline (Current)
- Establish the definition of "improvement" in a constrained domain (e.g., small coding tasks).
- Build the [Improvement Verification Harness](../../projects/improvement-verification/README.md) to measure baseline capabilities and evaluator robustness.

## Phase 2: Adaptation Mechanisms
- Implement specific adaptation strategies (e.g., self-reflection, strategy updates from environment feedback).
- Measure generalization vs. memorization using the harness from Phase 1.

## Phase 3: Mitigation and Robustness
- Develop techniques to detect and prevent evaluator gaming during the learning loop.
- Investigate memory management strategies to minimize capability regression over long horizons.
