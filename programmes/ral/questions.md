# RAL Research Questions

1.  **Verification**: How can we definitively distinguish between a system that has generalized a new skill and one that has merely memorized specific task distributions?
2.  **Evaluator Robustness**: What are the theoretical and practical limits of automated evaluators when used as learning signals for highly capable agents?
3.  **Continual Learning**: What architectural patterns best mitigate catastrophic forgetting in LLM-driven autonomous systems?
4.  **Epistemic Uncertainty**: How can an agent accurately assess its own competence boundary before and after an adaptation cycle?
